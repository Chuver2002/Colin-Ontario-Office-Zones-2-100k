If you lease an office here

  • Start 9–12 months out for suites under 10,000 SF, and 12–18 months for anything larger. Well-located small suites lease quickly.
  • Inland Empire office asking rents are rising slowly, about 2–5% a year: $2.11–$2.41/SF/mo full service across the research reports. In Ontario, recent leases asked a median $2.55. Know your number before the renewal letter arrives.
  • Ask for improvement dollars and free rent on longer terms. Recent Ontario comps include allowances like $20/SF on new leases.
  • Check what full service covers: parking, after-hours HVAC, janitorial and expense stops vary from building to building.

If you own a building here

  • 6 Ontario office buildings sold recently at a median $269/SF, and listed buildings are asking a median $375/SF.
  • Owner-users drive small-building demand: 2 of the 6 recent sales went to owner-users.
  • Inland Empire office vacancy is among the lowest in Southern California (4.9%–8.6% across the reports), so well-kept small suites lease.
  • Renewing a tenant early usually beats months of downtime plus a new tenant's improvements. Know your expirations and start talking 12 months out.

Buying in this zone

  • Listed office buildings in Ontario are asking a median $375/SF, against $269/SF on recent sales. Price against the sales, not the asks.
  • Small buildings and office condos are the owner-user sweet spot. Compare ownership cost to full-service rent before you decide.
  • Check parking ratio, HVAC age and roof condition early; they drive both value and tenant appeal in older buildings.
